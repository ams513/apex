II   Promises

Eleven things Stark can keep

Written as hard as the facts allow. If a promise holds for ten apps, the ten are named. An absolute with a hidden exception is a lie, so the exceptions sit under each one.

P2 Live

We cannot read your data. Not will not: cannot.

15 browser apps

Proof
The apps contain no code that sends anything anywhere. The only way out is a feedback button that builds an email your own mail app opens and you send. In Family Vault and Caregiver we read it: no record content. Homeroom, Baseline, Fellowship, Vital and Resonance build theirs the same way.
Test it in a minute
Open the browser's Network tab and use the app: zero requests. Search the js folder for fetch(: no match.

True of the app as shipped. A later version is a different file; compare them. E24, E25.

P3 Live

Forget the passphrase and nobody can open it. Not even us.

Family Vault, Ledger, Warden, Baseline, Fellowship, Vital

Proof
AES-GCM 256 under a key made from your passphrase. 600,000 PBKDF2 rounds in Family Vault and Warden. The product pages say: no reset, no recovery email, no back door.
Test it in a minute
Open Family Vault's js/core.js. Line 6 is KDF_ITER = 600000. Line 28 is AES-GCM, 256.

It cannot protect you from malware on your computer or a weak passphrase. Not Homeroom: its records sit unencrypted in the browser and only its backup vault is sealed. In Fellowship a Leader can set a new passphrase for another user. Not Resonance: it keeps plain settings in the browser and seals nothing. E5, E6, E36, E37.

III   The principle

Cultivate creation.
Never overcome.

That is the whole of Apex Protocol, in the owner's words, and it is a Principle The home page says it as Creation cultivated. Creator revered. Live

Why bind yourself with a limit you cannot lift

  1. A promise its maker can retract is a mood. A limit its maker cannot lift is a fact. People can build on a fact. Principle
  2. Software already has this kind of limit. A browser enforces a page's security policy, and the author of the page cannot talk it out of that while the page is open. Live
  3. The limit sits in a file you hold. To lift it, Stark would have to ship a different file, and you can compare the two. That is why we ask you to read it. Live
  4. The whole-company version, a rule that holds even against the founder, is his stated intent. It is not yet a mechanism anyone can audit, and we will not describe it as one. Principle
